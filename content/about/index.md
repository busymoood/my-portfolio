## Who I am

I'm Julien Fichet. I spend most of my time close to the hardware: C libraries, firmware, communication and security protocols, and the build and test tooling that keeps them honest.

I'm fascinated by cryptography and everything related to cybersecurity and self-sovereignty. I believe in open-source solutions that you can self-host and truly own.

## What I do

Since 2024 I have been an embedded software engineer at **Cogelec** (LEGRAND Group), where I work on a C library for a new system architecture, implement cryptographic features (AES, SHA, RSA, ECC) across STM32 platforms, and maintain the GitLab CI/CD pipelines around them.

## What I work with

* **Languages** — C, C++, Rust, Python  
* **Embedded** — STM32, ESP32, Zephyr, FreeRTOS
* **Security** — AES, SHA, RSA, ECC
* **Tooling** — Git, GitLab CI/CD, GitHub Actions, Docker, CMake, GDB/OpenOCD, Neovim

## Personal projects

- **[Relic Core](/projects/relic-core)** — An offline ESP32 device that splits secrets using Shamir's Secret Sharing and packages them into handy QR codes that you can share easily.
- **[Relic Hardware Guide](/projects/relic-hw)** — a beginner-friendly guide to building and using the Relic device.
- **[OvalBot](/projects/ovalbot)** — a Rust ticket-resale bot watcher and notifier to get seat seeing your favourite TOP14 rugby match.
